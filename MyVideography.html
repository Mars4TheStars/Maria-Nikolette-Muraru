Silent Film

Accompanying poem of the video:
In Life's Chaotic Dance by Maria-Nikolette Muraru


In the chaos of life, we whirl and twirl,
A fleeting dance, a transient swirl.
Moments, like whispers, swiftly pass,
Time slips away, too quick, alas.

Dreams and hopes, lost in the breeze,
We chase tomorrow, as today does not appease.
Life's tapestry, woven with unsolved riddles,
A masterpiece it could be, but rigged with missiles.

The ticking clock, its relentless chime,
Each second gone by, a stolen rhyme.
Silent footsteps of the unwelcomed guest,
Time's sand fills up the coffin slowly, for an easy rest.

We journey on, not knowing when,
The final act arrives, when the curtain will descend.
Cherish the chaos, the beauty, the strife,
For in the blink of an eye, it's the end of life.
